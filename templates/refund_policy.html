Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 01-08-2026

This policy explains our approach to order cancellations, returns, and refunds. Please read it before placing an order.

1. All Sales Are Final

All purchases made through Salted Quail are final. By placing an order, you acknowledge and agree that no returns, exchanges, or refunds will be issued once an order has been placed, for any reason, including but not limited to change of mind, incorrect size or colour selection, or delivery delays.

2. Order Cancellations

Once an order is placed and payment is confirmed, it cannot be cancelled or modified. Please review your cart, selected options (such as size or colour), and shipping details carefully before completing checkout.

3. Returns

We do not accept returns on any item.

4. Refunds

We do not offer refunds on any item, for any reason.

5. Shipping Costs

Shipping and handling fees are non-refundable.

6. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Visit our Contact Us page.
